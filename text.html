Franz Kafka's The Metamorphosis follows Gregor Samsa, a traveling salesman who unexpectedly transforms into a giant insect. Burdened by his family's financial reliance on him, Gregor struggles not with his bizarre physical change, but with the immediate fear of missing work. As he remains confined to his room, his family grows increasingly resentful of his condition and the strain it places on them. Ultimately, neglected and injured, Gregor starves to death, leaving his family with a chilling sense of relief and a hopeful outlook on their unburdened future.
